Trade & commissions

Fibonacci Landscapes is available to corporate art consultants, architecture and design practices, hospitality projects, property developers, and environmental organisations. Prints can be produced at architectural scale; commissions responding to specific sites or briefs are welcomed.

Working with the studio

Editioned prints are available in a range of sizes on archival papers. Larger-scale works, custom sizes, and framing options can be arranged. Site-responsive commissions — landscapes selected or made specifically for a project — are quoted on request.

A capabilities document is available on request.
